\chapter*{{\emph\LARGE{Abstract}}}

The increasing adoption of Industrial Internet of Things (IIoT) technologies has created a growing need for flexible and interoperable platforms capable of acquiring, processing, and exchanging data from a wide variety of sensors and industrial equipment. However, integrating heterogeneous devices often requires significant development effort due to the diversity of communication interfaces and protocols involved.

This end-of-study project was carried out within Be Wireless Solutions (BWS) and focuses on the development of the IO-Box, a configurable industrial Input/Output platform designed to simplify the integration of sensors, actuators, and supervisory systems. The platform provides data acquisition, signal processing, control, and communication functionalities through a modular and scalable architecture.

The work conducted during this project included the development of embedded software components, peripheral drivers, communication interfaces, and sensor management modules. Several industrial communication protocols were implemented and validated, enabling reliable interaction with external devices. Particular attention was given to software modularity, hardware abstraction, and system maintainability in order to facilitate future extensions of the platform.

The resulting solution offers a versatile and reusable architecture suitable for industrial monitoring, automation, and IoT applications. By reducing integration complexity and improving interoperability, the IO-Box contributes to the development of more efficient and scalable industrial systems.

\textbf{Keywords:} Industrial IoT, Embedded Systems, IO-Box, Data Acquisition, Industrial Communication Protocols, Sensors, Automation, Software Architecture.
